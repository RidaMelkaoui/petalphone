Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Petalphone is designed for people sharing one phone. It does not require an account, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

Game data

Player names, drawings, guesses, custom prompts, settings, and finished game history are stored on your device. Petalphone does not upload this game data to us. You can delete it from Settings inside the app. Removing the app also removes its local game data, subject to your device backup settings.

Purchases

If you buy or restore Full Garden, Apple or Google processes the payment. Petalphone uses RevenueCat to confirm the purchase and keep the unlock working. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user identifier, product identifier, purchase status, store, country, and related transaction information. We do not receive your full card or bank details.

RevenueCat's own privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

Petalphone does not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone recordings, or account profiles. We do not sell personal information or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Age

Petalphone is a general audience app rated for ages 13 and up. It is not directed to children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated date and text will appear on this page.

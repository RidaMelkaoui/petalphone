# App Review notes

Petalphone is a local pass-and-play drawing game. It does not require an account, internet connection for gameplay, or a second device.

## How to test the main game

1. Tap Start a game.
2. Choose 2 to 8 players and enter names.
3. Choose a deck, number of rounds, and pace.
4. Pass the device only when the private pass screen asks you to.
5. Read the prompt, draw it, and continue alternating guesses and drawings.
6. View the complete chain on the reveal screen.

For a quick review, choose 2 players, 1 round, and Quick pace. Cozy pace has no timer.

## Privacy and device behavior

Player names, drawings, guesses, custom prompts, settings, and finished games are stored locally. Delete local game data is available in Settings. The purchase entitlement remains restorable through the store account.

The app contains no ads, ad SDKs, accounts, analytics SDKs, tracking, user-generated content sharing, or unrestricted web browser. External links open only the Petalphone privacy, terms, and support pages.

The app is designed for phones. iPad support is disabled and no iPad screenshots should be submitted.

# Store privacy answers

These answers must be checked against the final dependency list before submission.

## Shared declarations

- Ads: No
- Account creation: No
- Tracking across apps or websites: No
- User-generated content upload or sharing: No
- Location: No
- Contacts: No
- Photos or media library: No
- Camera: No
- Microphone: No
- Health, financial, or sensitive data: No
- Crash or analytics SDK intentionally added by Petalphone: No

## Local-only game data

Player names, drawings, guesses, custom prompts, settings, and game history stay on the device. They are not sent to the developer.

## Purchase verification

Apple or Google processes payment. RevenueCat verifies Full Garden and may process an anonymous app user identifier, product identifier, purchase status, store, country, and related transaction information. Declare the final RevenueCat data categories exactly as required by the current App Store Connect and Google Play forms.

## Age and content

Petalphone is a general-audience game for ages 13 and up. Built-in prompts avoid sexual content, drugs, gambling, realistic violence, medical content, and strong profanity. Custom prompts stay on the device and are entered by the people using that device.

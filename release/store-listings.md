## Full description

Petalphone is a cozy drawing telephone game for people in the same room.

Pick your players and a prompt deck. The first person reads a private prompt and draws it. Pass the phone. The next person guesses the doodle, then someone else draws that guess. At the end, everyone sees how the idea changed along the way.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Play with 2 to 8 people on one phone
• Choose 1, 3, or 5 rounds
• Play without a timer, or choose a faster pace
• Draw with pen, erase, undo, redo, and clear
• Save finished games in your local garden
• Play without accounts, ads, or online matchmaking

The free game includes 120 prompts, every player count, every pace, and all drawing tools.

FULL GARDEN

Full Garden is one optional lifetime purchase. It adds 480 prompts in eight decks, custom prompt decks, five extra paper themes, more Bloomlings, and extra reveal styles.

Your names, drawings, guesses, custom prompts, settings, and finished games stay on your device. Purchase processing is handled by Apple or Google and verified through RevenueCat.
